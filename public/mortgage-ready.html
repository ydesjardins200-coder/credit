Preparing for a mortgage

Get your credit mortgage-ready.

A mortgage is likely the biggest application of your life, and your credit is front and center. A stronger profile can mean the difference between approval and rejection — and between a rate that costs you, and one that doesn’t. iBoost helps you prepare in the months before you apply.

Why it matters

Your credit shapes the whole deal

Mortgage lenders don’t just check whether you’ll be approved — your credit profile influences the rate you’re offered, the size of deposit you may need, and how much house you can afford. Small improvements before you apply can have an outsized effect over a 25- or 30-year loan.

The catch is timing. Credit doesn’t change overnight; it responds to steady, consistent activity over months. The best time to start preparing is well before you start house-hunting — not the week you find the place you love.

iBoost helps you use that runway. We help you build positive history and keep your profile clean in the lead-up to your application, with guidance on what mortgage lenders tend to look at.
