Rebuilding after a setback

A setback isn’t the end. It’s a starting point.

Missed payments, collections, or bankruptcy can feel like a door slamming shut. They’re not. Credit is rebuildable — it takes consistency and time, not magic. iBoost gives you a clear, judgment-free path forward.

You’re not alone

Rebuilding is normal — and doable

Setbacks happen to millions of responsible people — a job loss, a medical bill, a divorce, a business that didn’t work out. The damage to your credit is real, but it isn’t permanent, and it doesn’t define you.

Rebuilding works by layering new, positive history on top of the old. Over time, steady on-time activity carries more weight and the impact of past problems fades. The key is starting — and then staying consistent.
